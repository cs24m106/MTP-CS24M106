\subsection{ViMo-Flow Applications}
\label{sec:applications}

The ViMo-Flow framework enables three downstream applications that demonstrate its generative capabilities.

\subsubsection{3D Motion Dataset Construction}
The pipeline processes casual video to generate paired 2D--3D motion data at scale. Applied to Chinese classical dance competition videos, it produces temporally coherent motion clips spanning genres absent from existing corpora~\cite{h3wb2014, amass2019, act2mo2020}, enabling scalable dataset expansion from freely available online footage.

\subsubsection{Few-Shot Dancing Stylization}
Pre-trained music-to-motion models (e.g., EDGE~\cite{edge2023}) are limited to the dance categories seen during training. Zero-convolution adaptation blocks learn style-specific residuals while keeping the pre-trained backbone frozen, enabling transfer to unseen dance styles with minimal data and without catastrophic forgetting~\cite{edge2023}.

\subsubsection{Video-Guided Motion Completion}
Masked denoising supports three editing tasks: motion in-betweening, in-filling, and style blending~\cite{singshot2021, robmo2020}. A binary mask $\mathbf{b}$ designates known frames; at each reverse diffusion step, denoised estimates merge with noised constraints in masked regions via RePaint-style blending~\cite{repaint2022}:
\[
    \hat{\mathbf{m}}_{t-1} = \mathbf{b}\odot\overline{q(\mathbf{c}_{\text{const}},t=1)} + (1-\mathbf{b})\odot\hat{\mathbf{m}}_{t-1}.
\]
